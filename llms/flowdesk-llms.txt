# Flowdesk

> Flowdesk is a regulated full-service digital-asset trading and technology firm providing liquidity, OTC execution, and market infrastructure. It publishes the "Platform Connect" developer API for programmatic OTC spot trading over REST plus a WebSocket streaming channel, authenticated with OAuth 2.0 JWT bearer tokens issued through its Auth0 tenant.
